# Art bible

Owned by the Director as Art Director; every direction here is a choice the
designer approved. Proposals live in reviews until approved. Keep it short enough
to read before any review.

Placeholder phase: no visual direction chosen. Until then, apply only the
readability rules below and the current build prompt or ticket.

## Readability rules (apply in every phase)

- The player's current goal, selection, and next valid action are visible at the
  gameplay camera distance without reading text.
- Interactive and non-interactive elements differ in shape or value, not hue alone.
- State changes that matter (loaded, damaged, delivered, blocked) have a visible
  signal that survives a grayscale check.
- Text is legible at the target resolution and UI scale.
